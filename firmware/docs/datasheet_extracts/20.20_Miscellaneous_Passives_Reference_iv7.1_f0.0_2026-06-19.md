### Critical firmware/PCB design note — Load Capacitance
**The CL variant ordered determines which external caps to place.** The PCF85063A oscillator's effective load capacitance is set by the two PCB capacitors (C_ext1, C_ext2) in series plus stray capacitance (~2–3 pF typical for a short trace). General formula: CL = (C_ext1 × C_ext2)/(C_ext1 + C_ext2) + C_stray.

For the common 12.5 pF crystal with ~2.5 pF stray: two matched caps of ~20 pF each in series give approximately (20×20)/(20+20) + 2.5 = 12.5 pF. Verify actual CL variant on the BOM. If the 6 pF variant was ordered, caps should be approximately 7 pF each (series = 3.5 pF + 2.5 pF stray).

The datasheet does not specify which CL variant the NTLLLDT order code corresponds to — the ordering code suffix must be decoded or confirmed with HCI. [p. 1]

### Stability calculation
At body temperature ~35°C (ΔT = 10°C above turning point 25°C):
- Frequency deviation = −0.03 × 10² = −3 ppm. At 32.768 kHz this is ~0.1 Hz/s drift, or about 8.6 s/day — well within RTC correction range. The PCF85063A has a software offset register to correct this.

### Application on Kompic Mk I
The ESDALCL5-1BM2 is a single-line device. On a wearable with multiple I2C lines and interrupt lines, each protected line requires one device. The system instructions do not explicitly identify which specific signals use this device vs. the TPD2E001.

**I2C signal integrity concern:** The 26 pF typ (30 pF max) line capacitance is significant. At I2C Fast Mode 400 kHz with 5.1 kΩ pull-ups and 3.2 V rail, the RC time constant is 5100 × 30×10⁻¹² = 153 ns. The I2C spec allows maximum rise time of 300 ns at 400 kHz (C_bus ≤ 400 pF, t_r ≤ 300 ns). A single ESDALCL5-1BM2 contributes 30 pF max per line. If multiple devices are daisy-chained on a bus that also has node capacitance, verify total C_bus remains under 400 pF. If used only at the connector/port entry point (not mid-bus), impact is limited. Datasheet recommends placement at the input of the device under protection. [p. 1]

**No firmware action required.** This is a passive protection device with no register interface.

### Application on Kompic Mk I — USB D+/D-

### Charging circuit on Kompic Mk I
From system instructions: "ML621 rechargeable cell (5.5 mAh), 1×1N4148 + 10 kΩ charge limit. At the PCF85063's sub-µA timekeeping draw this holds the clock for ~4 years." [02_Kompic_Mk1_System_Instructions_v7.1.md]

**Critical discrepancy — charge voltage:**
- The 3.2 V rail charges through one 1N4148 diode (V_f ≈ 0.6–0.7 V forward). At 10 µA (the level in the RTC backup circuit), V_f for a 1N4148 is approximately 0.5–0.55 V (much lower than the 0.7 V at 10 mA).
- Cell charge voltage ≈ 3.2 V − 0.5 V = 2.7 V — this falls BELOW the 2.8 V minimum guaranteed charging voltage.
- At 2.7 V the datasheet warns: "It is not possible for the battery to recover completely when the charging voltage is below the specification." [p. 2]
- **Action required:** Increase charge voltage to ensure cell sees ≥ 2.8 V. Options: (a) use the Schottky diode instead of 1N4148 (V_f ≈ 0.2–0.3 V at low current → cell gets 2.9–3.0 V); (b) use 3.3 V rail if available as charge source; (c) verify actual 3.2 V rail voltage and actual diode drop at the circuit's charge current. The BQ25619's REGN reference (~3.3–3.5 V) could be an alternative source if accessible.

**Trickle charging prohibition:** The current Kompic charging circuit (1N4148 + 10 kΩ from 3.2 V) is a passive constant-current-limiting resistor plus diode, not a true CV charger. It approximates CV because the cell voltage will rise until it equals (V_rail − V_diode), at which point current drops to approximately zero. This is acceptable as a passive CV approximation. The prohibition on trickle charging refers to forcing current into a fully-charged cell, which this circuit avoids because the diode + resistor current naturally falls as the cell charges up. However, if the cell voltage ever exceeds the rail-minus-diode voltage, the diode blocks reverse current — correct behavior.

### RTC backup duration calculation
The PCF85063A (from its datasheet, not in this extract) has a timekeeping current of approximately 0.25 µA typical in backup mode.

- ML621 capacity: 5.0 mAh
- PCF85063A backup current: ~0.25 µA (estimate — verify from 20.04 PCF85063A extract)
- Backup duration (ignoring self-discharge): 5.0 mAh / 0.00025 mA = 20,000 hours = ~833 days = ~2.3 years
- Self-discharge: ML621 self-discharge at room temperature is not explicitly stated as a rate. At 20°C the continuous-charging chart shows nearly 100% retention over 300 days at 3.1 V. Without charging, the cell will self-discharge much faster — a conservative estimate for Li-Mn coin cells is ~1–3% per year at 25°C, but this is not stated in the datasheet.
- System instructions state "~4 years" as an order-of-magnitude estimate. This is plausible if the cell is frequently recharged during normal watch operation, but 4 years without any charge from the main rail is optimistic.
- **Backup behavior when main battery is removed:** the cell will drain at ~0.25 µA. 5 mAh / 0.00025 mA = 20,000 h ≈ 833 days ≈ 2.3 years from full charge. This assumes no other leakage paths.
- **Capacity discrepancy:** System instructions reference "5.5 mAh" for the ML621. Datasheet specifies 5.0 mAh. Use 5.0 mAh for calculations.

**None.** The XC6206 is a 3-terminal device (V_SS, V_IN, V_OUT — no enable pin). Output is active whenever V_IN is present and above dropout. [p. 2–3]

Since the XC6206 VIN is fed from the TPS62840 3.2 V output, and the TPS62840 is always-on (EN = SYS rail), the 1.8 V rail is always on whenever the battery/USB is connected. There is no FW-controlled shutdown of the 1.8 V rail.

### Capacitor requirements
- Input capacitor: 1.0 µF ceramic minimum (place close to IC). [p. 1, p. 6]
- Output capacitor: 1.0 µF ceramic minimum. [p. 1, p. 6]
- Low ESR capacitors: ceramic. Compatible with MLCC. [p. 1]

### Dropout check
V_IN = 3.2 V (from TPS62840), V_OUT = 1.8 V. Headroom = 1.4 V. Dropout voltage at 30 mA is 150 mV typical, 390 mV max. Headroom is ample — no dropout risk even at max current draw.

## Open questions

1. **ML621 charge voltage:** What is the actual measured voltage at the ML621 terminals during normal operation? Given the 3.2 V rail and the 1N4148 V_f at the micro-amp charge current, the cell may be receiving < 2.8 V. Measure in circuit before trusting RTC backup longevity. Consider replacing with a Schottky diode.

2. **HCI FC31M2 CL variant:** What is the specific load capacitance (6, 9, or 12.5 pF) of the crystal variant ordered? The NTLLLDT suffix does not decode unambiguously from the datasheet. Confirm with HCI or the LCSC listing, then verify PCB cap values match.

3. **ESDALCL5-1BM2 application mapping:** Which specific signals on the Kompic PCB use ESDALCL5-1BM2 vs no ESD protection? The single-line device would logically be used on I2C lines at the connector, interrupt lines, or the USB CC lines (though CC uses 5.1 kΩ to GND which provides some protection). Confirm placement to assess capacitive loading budget.

4. **TPD2E001 package variant:** Which package variant (SOT-5X3 DRL, USON DRY, WSON DRS, or SOT-23 DZD) is fitted? The LCSC part number should clarify this. The 1.5 pF capacitance is package-independent for the electrical spec.

5. **TPD2E001 V_CC connection:** Is V_CC connected to 3.3 V (with 0.1 µF bypass) or left as a floating bypass cap only? For USB CDC at 3.3 V IO levels, V_CC to 3.3 V is the cleaner configuration. Confirm in schematic.

6. **GPS antenna keep-out and epoxy window alignment:** Is the ceramic patch antenna's radiating face correctly aimed through the epoxy window in the titanium case? The patch must have an unobstructed sky-facing window. Standard clear epoxy is RF-transparent; opaque filled epoxy may add loss.

7. **BLE/GPS 50 Ω trace width:** When is the stackup going to be characterised and traces re-routed? This blocks reliable RF performance for both antennas. Should be addressed before the first RF test.

8. **ML621 capacity discrepancy:** The system instructions reference "5.5 mAh" for the ML621, but the datasheet specifies 5.0 mAh. Use the datasheet 5.0 mAh for calculations.

9. **1.8V rail sensor sleep currents:** Confirm all 1.8V-domain sensors (MAX30101 VDDA, BME688, LSM6DSV16X V_IO, LIS3MDLTR VDD_IO, GPS V_IO, mic VDD) are correctly configured to enter low-power or shutdown mode in firmware sleep, since the XC6206 has no enable pin and the rail cannot be hardware-killed.

10. **GPS backup supercap vs ML621 in 20.08 vs this extract:** The 20.08 extract documented the GPS supercap hold time as ~2 hours. The system instructions say ~2 hours hot-start hold. The ML621 is solely for RTC (PCF85063A) backup; it does not back up the GPS. Confirm no cross-wiring between these two isolated backup domains.
