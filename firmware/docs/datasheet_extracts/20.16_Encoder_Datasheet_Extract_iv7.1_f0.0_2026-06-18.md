## Critical findings (read this first)

No hardware-breaking conflicts vs. iv7.1. Three firmware-actionable points:

### 1. Bounce time is 6 ms — PCNT filter must be supplemented with software debounce [p. 3]

The datasheet states: *"振荡 : 6ms max. 跳动 : 6ms max."* (oscillation bounce: 6 ms max; jitter bounce: 6 ms max) measured at V=5V, R=10kΩ. Both figures are quoted at 5V. At 3.3V with pull-ups, bounce may differ; however, the datasheet gives no 3.3V figure. **Use 6 ms as the design value.** The ESP32-S3 PCNT glitch filter register `PCNT_GLITCH_THRES` is 10 bits (max 1023 cycles = ~12.8 µs at 80 MHz APB), which is **far too short to filter mechanical bounce on its own.** Mechanical debounce must be handled in firmware (timer-based or state-machine) after the PCNT interrupt; the PCNT filter only eliminates RF/EMI glitches.

### 2. Output is purely resistive (mechanical switch) — 3.3V compatible with pull-ups [p. 1, p. 3]

The encoder is a passive mechanical type. The terminals (A, B, C) are dry contacts — no active output stage, no power supply required by the encoder itself. The rated operating point is 0.55 mA @ 5.5V DC. At 3.3V with 10kΩ pull-ups the contact current is 3.3V / 10kΩ = 0.33 mA, within the 0.55 mA rating. 3.3V operation is confirmed safe.

### 3. A/B phase at detent position is undefined [p. 3]

The output waveform diagram includes a note: *"※ 定位位置の A 相、B 相输出未作规定。"* — "The A-phase and B-phase outputs at the detent position are not specified." Firmware must not rely on a known absolute A/B level at rest; it must decode transitions only.

## Firmware implications

### PCNT configuration

The ESP32-S3 PCNT hardware filter (`PCNT_GLITCH_THRES`) can only suppress pulses shorter than ~12.8 µs at 80 MHz APB. The encoder's 6 ms bounce is ~470× longer than the filter maximum — **the PCNT filter alone cannot debounce this encoder.**

Recommended approach:

1. **PCNT glitch filter:** Set to maximum (~1023 APB cycles ≈ 12.8 µs) to suppress RF/EMI glitches only.

2. **Interrupt-driven debounce timer:** On PCNT interrupt, start a one-shot FreeRTOS timer for **8 ms** (6 ms bounce + 2 ms margin). Discard any additional PCNT events that arrive within the 8 ms window. Accept the first event after the timer expires as a confirmed tick.

3. **Quadrature decoding mode:** Use PCNT in quadrature mode. At 12 PPR with X1 → 12 counts/rev (1 count per detent click — natural UI unit).

4. **Direction:** PCNT natively detects direction from A/B phase sequence. Check CW/CCW polarity on bench and swap A/B assignment in firmware if needed (no hardware change required).

5. **Boot edge on GPIO43 (EC_SigB):** A single ~3 ms spurious edge appears on TXD0/GPIO43 during the boot ROM log. Delay PCNT init by ≥10 ms post-boot to avoid it.

6. **Wake from deep sleep:** GPIO21 (EC_SigA) is RTC wake-capable; GPIO43 (EC_SigB) is not — direction cannot be determined on the wake edge alone. Direction decoding resumes after full init.

### Suggested PCNT register values (ESP-IDF v5.x)

```c
pcnt_unit_config_t unit_cfg = {
    .low_limit  = -32767,
    .high_limit =  32767,
};
pcnt_glitch_filter_config_t filter_cfg = {
    .max_glitch_ns = 12800,  // 12.8 µs max — suppresses EMI only, not mechanical bounce
};
// Software 8 ms one-shot debounce timer in PCNT ISR callback
```

## Datasheet notes

1. **Chinese-language datasheet** (ALPS Alpine product page, archived 2017-10-25). Part EC05E1220401 = "5-type through-shaft" (5型贯通轴型) EC05E series. [p. 1]
2. **Bounce measurement conditions:** oscillation and jitter both measured at 5V DC, R=10kΩ, rotation speed 360°/s. [p. 3]
3. **A/B waveform:** Standard 2-phase quadrature, overlapping pulses 90° apart, CW rotation shown. At each detent, A/B output state explicitly not guaranteed. [p. 3]
4. **Reflow conditions:** Peak 250°C max, 230°C min for soldering zone, preheat 150–180°C for 60–120s, peak dwell 30–40s, max 2 reflow passes. Hand-solder: ≤350°C iron, 3s max. [p. 4]
5. **Shaft hex profile:** 6-60° lobe pattern, B-face reference ø2.7 mm — crown shaft must be machined to mate with this geometry for the slip fit. [p. 2]
6. **Lifespan in context:** 100,000 cycles at 12 detents/rev = 8,333 full revolutions. At 20 crown interactions/day ≈ 1.5 years to wear rating. Relevant for serviceability planning. [p. 1]

## Open questions

1. **Contact resistance value:** Not stated in datasheet. Measure on bench with 4-wire milliohm measurement (expected < 100Ω for a new encoder). Relevant if pull-up resistor value is changed.
2. **Bounce at 3.3V:** The 6 ms spec is at 5V. Measure bounce on bench with 3.3V supply and scope before finalising the debounce timer value.
3. **CW/CCW polarity mapping:** Confirm on bench — "CW" is relative to encoder body orientation on PCB. Adjust PCNT channel count direction in firmware if inverted.
