## Protocol — RMT Timing (CRITICAL)

Source: "Data Transfer Time" table [p. 4] and timing diagram [p. 5].

| Symbol | Description | Min | Typ | Max | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T0H | 0-bit high time | 220 | — | 380 | ns |
| T0L | 0-bit low time | 580 | — | 1000 | ns |
| T1H | 1-bit high time | 580 | — | 1000 | ns |
| T1L | 1-bit low time | 580 | — | 1000 | ns |
| TRESET (RES) | Frame unit, low voltage (reset/latch) | 280 | — | — | µs |

Constraint [p. 4]: T0H+T0L ≥ 1.25 µs and T1H+T1L ≥ 1.25 µs. Nominal 800 Kbps [p. 1].

Reset note [p. 1]: "RESET time > 280 µs — won't cause wrong reset while interruption." Use ≥ 300 µs in RMT driver for margin.

Recommended RMT values (ESP32-S3, APB 80 MHz): T0H=300ns, T0L=900ns, T1H=750ns, T1L=600ns, RESET=300µs low.

## Data format

Source: "Composition of 24bit data" [p. 5].

- **Bits per LED:** 24 bits
- **Color order: GRB** — G7..G0 first, R7..R0 second, B7..B0 third [p. 5]
- **Bit order: MSB first** — "high bit data at first" [p. 5]
- **Gray levels:** 256 per channel, 8-bit [p. 1]

**Firmware:** To set color (R, G, B), transmit bytes [G, R, B] MSB-first. G and R are swapped vs natural RGB byte order.

## Logic level compatibility

Source: Electrical Characteristics [p. 3].

| Parameter | Symbol | Value | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| VIH minimum | VIH | **0.65 × VDD = 3.25V** | V |
| VIL maximum | VIL | 0.7V | V |

**ESP32-S3 GPIO42 VOH ≈ 2.9–3.1V** from the 3.2V TPS62840 rail (Kompic runs 3.2V, not 3.3V). This is **below VIH(min) = 3.25V. Zero margin. INCOMPATIBLE as-is.**

Recommended fix: add a 3.3V→5V single-channel level shifter (e.g. SN74LVC1T45, SOT-363) on the DIN line. Flag for v7.2 schematic correction. Alternatively bench-verify explicitly — if it works in practice with the 47Ω series resistor, document it, but do not assume.

## Datasheet notes

1. **No filter capacitor needed** [p. 1]: Datasheet explicitly states no bypass cap required on VDD. A 100nF cap is harmless if placed anyway.
2. **Reverse polarity protection** [p. 1]: IC survives reversed VDD. VDD can withstand 12V in static state.
3. **Signal reshaping** [p. 1, p. 5]: Each pixel reshapes and re-drives DO. For single LED, DO is NC.
4. **MSL 5a** [p. 8]: Workshop life 24 hours after unpacking at ≤30°C/60%RH. Bake before reflow (70–75°C × ≥24h whole reel, or 120°C × 4h bulk).
5. **Reflow profile** [p. 9]: Lead-free. Peak 245°C, reflow 45–90s, liquid region above 217°C. Compatible with standard board fab profile.
6. **Datasheet version V1.5**, revised 2022-08-23 [p. 10].

## Open questions

1. **Level shifter on DIN:** Does v7.2 schematic include a 3.3V→5V translator on WS2812 DIN? If not, add to BOM (SN74LVC1T45 or equivalent). Verify before finalising v7.2.
2. **Actual per-channel drive current:** Datasheet gives 12 mA as test condition, not a hard spec. Measure on bench at VDD=5V, full white, to confirm battery budget figure.
3. **PMID boost sequencing:** BQ25619 PMID boost and charger are mutually exclusive. Firmware must enable boost before activating the LED and coordinate with charging state. Not a WS2812B issue but gates LED use.
4. **LED brightness for indicator:** At full white/36 mA the LED will be intensely bright at wrist distance. Plan to use PWM values of ~5–30/255 for normal indicator use. Calibrate at bring-up.
5. **GPIO42 boot transients:** GPIO42 = MTMS (JTAG strap). If 5V PMID boost is not yet enabled during boot, the LED is unpowered and any boot transient on GPIO42 is irrelevant. Confirm PMID enable sequencing in firmware boot sequence to ensure this safe ordering.
